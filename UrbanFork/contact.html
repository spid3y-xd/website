Essential Dining Policies

Key details to ensure your evening unfolds with effortless elegance.

Our attire standard is Smart Elegant. Tailored jackets are encouraged for gentlemen though not strictly mandatory; collared shirts and tailored trousers are required. We kindly prohibit athletic sportswear, hoodies, flip-flops, and baseball caps.
