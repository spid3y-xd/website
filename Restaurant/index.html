The Urban Fork Ethos

Quiet luxury sculpted in raw stone and live embers.

Conceived as a sanctuary from metropolitan tempo, Urban Fork pairs monolithic basalt counters and brushed brass with understated acoustic warmth. We believe fine dining should be profound yet unpretentious, grounding each patron in a multisensory celebration of seasonality.

“Gastronomy is an architectural dialogue between raw nature and human contemplation.”

Executive Chef Marcus Vance · Master Sommelier Hélène Laurent
