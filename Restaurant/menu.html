The Signature Journey

The Seven-Course Autumn Progression

A blind tasting menu conceived daily by Chef Marcus Vance based on that morning's foraged deliveries and dockside catches. Includes amuse-bouches, coastal prelude, handmade pasta, live-fire cut, palate cleansing botanical granita, and chocolate finish.
